The Mission

Built For The Community.

Star FM 102.9 is not a corporate station. We are owned by, built for, and powered by the community of Klerksdorp and the Southern District. Every broadcast is an act of empowerment.

🏘️
Hyperlocal Voices
We platform local leaders, entrepreneurs, educators, and activists — giving a microphone to the people making real change.
📚
Education First
School information, adult literacy, bursary alerts, and skills training — education content runs every single day.
💼
Economic Empowerment
Tender opportunities, SMME resources, government grants and business tips that help listeners build real wealth.
🎵
Culture & Identity
Local music, indigenous languages, cultural heritage and stories that remind our community who they are.
🤝
Social Cohesion
A place where all community members — regardless of background — come to listen, learn and belong.
🌍
A New Model for Radio
Modern, digital-first, and fundable — a community radio model worth scaling across South Africa.
