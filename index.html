The Mission

Built For The Community

Star FM 102.9 is not a corporate station. We are owned by, built for, and powered by the community of Klerksdorp and the Dr Kenneth Kaunda District of North West.

🏘️
Hyperlocal Voices
Local leaders, entrepreneurs, educators, activists — a microphone for the people making real change.
📚
Education First
School information, bursary alerts, adult literacy, and skills training — every single day on air.
💼
Economic Empowerment
Tender alerts, SMME resources, government grants, and business tips that build real wealth.
🎵
Culture & Identity
Local music, indigenous languages, cultural heritage — the stories that remind us who we are.
🤝
Social Cohesion
A place where all community members come to listen, learn, and belong — regardless of background.
🌍
A New Model for Radio
Modern, digital-first, fundable — a community radio model worth scaling across South Africa.
