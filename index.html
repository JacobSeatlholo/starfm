The Mission

Built For The
Community.

Star FM 102.9 is not a corporate station. We are owned by, built for, and powered by the community of Klerksdorp and the Southern District of North West. Every broadcast is an act of empowerment.

🏘️
Hyperlocal Voices
We platform local leaders, entrepreneurs, educators, and activists — giving a microphone to the people who are making real change on the ground.
📚
Education First
From school information to adult literacy, bursary alerts to skills training — education content runs across our schedule every single day.
💼
Economic Empowerment
We broadcast tender opportunities, SMME resources, government grants and business tips that help our listeners generate income and build wealth.
🎵
Culture & Identity
Local music, indigenous languages, cultural heritage and stories that remind our community who they are and where they come from.
🤝
Social Cohesion
In a divided world, Star FM is a place where all community members — regardless of background — come to listen, learn and belong.
🌍
A New Model for Radio
We're proving that community radio can be modern, digital-first, and fundable — a model worth scaling across the rest of South Africa.
